# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** March 22, 2026

## Introduction

Bookmark Startpage ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome extension handles data.

## Data Collection

**We do not collect any personal data.**

The Bookmark Startpage extension:
- Does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information
- Does not track your browsing history
- Does not use analytics or tracking tools
- Does not display advertisements
- Does not communicate with external servers

## Data Storage

All data is stored **locally** in your browser:

- **Chrome Storage API**: Used to save your extension settings (tab configuration, preferences)
- **Bookmarks API**: Used to read and manage your Chrome bookmarks (as intended functionality)

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third parties.

## Permissions

The extension requires the following permissions:

### bookmarks
**Purpose:** To read your bookmark structure and display it on the start page, and to allow bookmark management (add, edit, delete) directly from the extension.

### storage
**Purpose:** To save your extension settings and tab configuration between browser sessions.

### favicon
**Purpose:** To display website icons next to bookmarks for visual recognition.

## Third-Party Services

This extension does not:
- Use third-party analytics
- Integrate with external APIs
- Share data with any external services
